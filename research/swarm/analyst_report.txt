is not a broad free-to-paid ladder from the middle segments. Indie and
skeptic are not a monetizable tier at launch.

4. OBJECTION CLUSTERING

Eight named clusters with counts and sharpest quote:

1. Reclaim/deletion trust and reversibility (28). Sharpest, P009: "You're
literally selling the thing I'm most afraid of: deleting my history."
This cluster dominates every segment, including the highest spenders.
2. Reference-host numbers don't apply to me (15). P016: "You quote me
97.7KB and 85.4% from some reference host that is not mine."
3. Build-it-myself: cron + du + find (12). P039: "I can write this in two
evenings with cron + du + find."
4. Wrong pain: agents forgetting decisions, not bytes (12). P069: "My
agents forget things because the loop drops decision context."
5. Not worth it: low spend, pocket change (12). P094: "You're selling me
a delete button for pocket change."
6. "No quality loss" unproven / self-reported (9). P025: "Your 'no
quality loss' claim is doing all the heavy lifting."
7. Security/compliance: write path disqualifies (6). P049: "The write
path disqualifies me."
8. Scope: two products in one skin (4). P015: "You're genuinely selling
two products in one skin."

(Minor residual: vendor credibility, 2, P021: "a hygiene tool for agent
stacks, built by agents.")

The sharpest pair is cluster 1 plus cluster 6: the two biggest pain
objections are both about the reclaim step being unprovable on the
buyer's own data. Deletion fear and unproven no-quality-loss are really
one meta-objection: "I will only trust reclaim if you prove it lossless
and reversible on MY stack first."

5. MISSING-FEATURE CLUSTERING

The single dominant demand, present in well over half of the 100 records:
run the census and the before/after benchmark read-only against the
buyer's OWN real session stores and show THEIR dollar figure and THEIR
losslessness, not a "reference host" number. That is the most-demanded
proof bar none; it is the entry condition for almost every persona
regardless of segment.

Named clusters:
- Proof on my actual stores / not a reference host, read-only dry-run
(dominant, roughly 55-60).
- Proven revert/restore/reversibility, actual restore test (11): P098 "A
real, demonstrated restore test, not a 'gated and reversible' line."
- Self-hosted / fully local / zero-trust / no-write-required mode (6):
P046 "A fully local/self-hosted mode."
- Audit artifact: diff ledger / manifest / signed provenance for clients
and compliance (5): P089 "show me the diff ledger."
- Key facts preserved under distillation (part of the lossless cluster,
folded in).
- Benchmark methodology transparency (2), actionable-not-scary dollar
card (3), security deployment spec (1).

The missing-feature story is the mirror of the objection story:
everything funnels to "prove the savings and the losslessness on my own
real store, read-only, before any write."

6. BIAS AUDIT

Clear inflation signals:

- The all-same-verdict structure. Seven segments at 100% TRY and exactly
one INSTALL across 100 personas is not a neutral result, it is the LLM
author, who knows the product, never permitting failure outside the
skeptic bloc. The skeptic bloc is the only place reality leaked in (33%
pull, 24.7 resonance, $0 median), and it is the only segment with any
IGNORE.

- Verdict/intonation mismatches. High-skepticism personas returning TRY
with real dollars and mid resonance:
P048 (skept 0.71) TRY $250, "you're a solo founder" objection;
P045 (skept 0.67) TRY $150;
P050 (skept 0.72) TRY $80;
these are the textbook "0.7-skeptic at TRY with a three-figure price,"
socially inflated.
P025 (skept 0.44) RES 85 PAY $99 is the single highest resonance in the
set and reads generous.
Within the skeptic bloc, P098 (skept 0.97) TRY RES 44, P089 (0.81) TRY
PAY 15, P099 (0.82) TRY RES 42 all contradict their stated 0.8-1.0
resistance and low pain; they are inflated residual. No skeptic hit
INSTALL, but any TRY on a 0.9+ skeptic and 1-2 pain is a mismatch.
P014 (pain 9, RES 84) returning PAY $0, and P005 (pain 8) PAY $0, are
lower-severity mismatches pushing in both directions.

Honesty haircut: overall install+try starts at 86%. Downgrading every
verdict one step (INSTALL to TRY, TRY to IGNORE, IGNORE unchanged)
collapses the install+try share to exactly 1% (the single former INSTALL,
now TRY). This is a deliberately punitive worst case, but it dramatizes
the real finding: the funnel is almost entirely soft TRY with no INSTALL
conviction to absorb even one step of skepticism, so any honest
correction is devastating. The verdict mix is not a go signal on its own.

7. IMPLICATIONS (three bullets)

- MVP: the only wedge that survives every objection is the genuinely
read-only census plus before/after benchmark run against the buyer's own
real session stores, delivering their own dollar card before any write;
reclaim and file-cleanup stay gated and secondary because they are the
two trust killers (deletion fear 28, lossless proof 9).

- Go decision: simulated intent is inflated and structurally hollow (86%
install+try but ~1% after a one-step haircut), so it cannot clear the
market gate on its own; the go decision still rests on real-world
activation (>=40% installs from an actual read-only census cohort), not
this panel.

- Target first: paid-heavy operators and enterprise, the only segments
with real dollars (median $25 and $60, top payers $99 and $500) and the
strongest cost-card resonance, entering strictly through the read-only
proof-on-my-stack wedge; indie (median $5) and skeptic (median $0) are a
negative-ROI first market.
