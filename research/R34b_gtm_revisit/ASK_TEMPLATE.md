# 1:1 receipt ask (Telegram DM, one person at a time)

Hey - built something new, and your machine is exactly what it's for.

sweap weighs your AI agent's memory store and tells you how much of it is
dead weight (duplicate reasoning, spent tool output). One command, 30
seconds, read-only, nothing leaves your machine:

curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nKOxxx/sweap.it/main/cli/weigh_cc.py | python3

Then paste me the output. That's it - your numbers go on the public
receipt board (with your handle or anonymous, your call):

https://sweap.it/#reportcard

On my machine it found 1,388 duplicate-reasoning rows in a 238 MB store,
and an earlier diet had already cut 37.57 MB with zero bytes of memory
lost. Curious what yours says.

# notes for the sender (founder)

- Send individually. Never as a group blast; the ask is personal.
- If they run it, watch for the receipt landing at
  github.com/nKOxxx/sweap.it/issues - the template is one click.
- The permission line in the template ("May we quote your numbers?")
  is the gate for putting their slip on the site. No yes = numbers
  only, no handle.
- If their output shows an error, that is ALSO valuable: ask for a
  screenshot, it becomes a fix. A failed run that turns into a fix is
  the best receipt of all.
