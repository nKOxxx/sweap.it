## THE DIAGNOSIS (worker, hub-endorsed)

Cleaning tools fail when the frame is "you are sick, take your medicine."
They escape by selling a DIFFERENT benefit the healthy already pay for:
(a) the shocking reveal (CCleaner's free scan), (b) protection from a
universal threat (antivirus, 1Password), (c) a status object (dashboards),
(d) identity the user is proud of (Duolingo streaks, flattering not
confessional). In every escapee, cleaning is the mechanism, never the message.

## THE VERDICT

**Primary: THE RECEIPT. Secondary: THE TRUST ANCHOR.**

- RECEIPT (primary): "Your AI billed you for work you never asked for;
  sweap shows what, and why, every time." Reaches every PAYING AI user,
  not the drowning; flattering to catch, not confessional to admit. Built
  on the one asset competitors cannot claim: net effect with named causes.
- TRUST ANCHOR (secondary): "physically cannot phone home; every byte
  stays on your machine." Bought by the healthy and cautious (the exact
  people the attack said we cannot reach); makes the receipt credible.
- Combined line: "Your AI wastes your money, we prove how, and none of it
  leaves your machine."
