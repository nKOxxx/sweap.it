## THE ATTACK WE ARE ANSWERING (from a red-team round, severity HIGH)

People who install a context-cleaning tool are self-selected: the heaviest,
most pain-drenched users. So the product's own headline numbers describe a
niche, and worse: nobody who is NOT already drowning wakes up searching for
a "cleaning tool." Hygiene is bought by the already-sick. Marketing a
cleaner as a cleaner may cap the product at the niche that already knows
it has the problem.

## THE COMPETITIVE FRAME (founder's own words)

- CAVEMAN sells a felt benefit: it SIMPLIFIES the output (speak less,
  compress request bytes in flight, ~111.9k stars [THEORY]). You see the
  difference immediately.
- JEV sells a felt benefit: it works FASTER and SKIPS the nonsense (typed
  decision model that filters what enters context [THEORY]). Speed is felt.
- SWEAP currently sells... hygiene. Store census, provably-dead sweep,
  wire ledger. Correct, measured, and nobody feels a database at rest.

## WHAT WE ACTUALLY HAVE (all [ANCHOR], use freely)

- 90.69% of stored transcript tokens are tool exhaust (exact census).
- One sweep: 14,955 dead rows, 19.56 MB, 48% of the store file, provably
  dead only, quarantine + SHA-256 + byte-identical restore.
- Wire ledger (no proxy): per-lane cache truth. Paid lane 96.3% hit; one
  token mutation drops a warm lane to 0%; 3 real breaks in one day =
  207,566 tokens billed at full price (2.02% of paid-lane input, ceiling
  333,120, net ~202,003). Each break attributed to a CAUSE from store
  records: one was a context compaction 95 seconds earlier, two were idle.
- Local-only by construction: product code contains zero network calls;
  every byte stays on the customer's machine.
- Works on day one with no integration: first run backfills the customer's
  entire break history automatically.
- The only one of the three tools that can state a NET effect (savings
  minus cache-break costs) instead of gross claims.

## MARKETING LAWS (fixed constraints, do not violate)

- No paid promotion, no cold outreach. Product-led distribution only:
  the product itself, its output, plugin directories, marketplaces,
  word of mouth.
- Every number public must be measured or clearly stamped [HYPOTHETICAL].
  No invented stats, no fake urgency.
- Prior positioning ruling: sweap is COMPLEMENTARY to caveman, never a
  competitor (fights on different planes).
- Prior lesson from caveman's rise [THEORY]: name = thesis, community
  becomes the marketing, there was a shareable mechanic (people replied
  in caveman-speak). Sweap needs its own shareable mechanic.
