## THE FRAME UNDER TEST (founder-approved, three layers)

LAYER 1 - THE JOKE (weight): "Your AI is fat. 57% of its memory is junk:
abandoned tool output, dead files, exhaust it hoards and never uses."
- The joke is on the AI, never the user (kills the sick-niche attack: not
  confessional, universal, funny).
- Entry action: `sweap weigh`, 2 seconds, read-only. Their own number.

LAYER 2 - THE STING (carrying charge): "And that junk is not sitting still.
Your AI drags it into every conversation. When it loses the thread (which
happens), it pays FULL PRICE to re-read all the junk too."
- Measured basis [ANCHOR]: one observed day had 207,566 tokens billed at
  full price from 3 memory-loss events; cache hit on the good lane is 96.3%,
  so sting days are real but not every day.
- This answers "so what?" for the weigh-in: the fat has a carrying charge.

LAYER 3 - THE RETENTION (trainer that stays): "Diets fail because they end.
Sweap stays on the scale." After the one-command diet (undoable), sweap keeps
watching: weighs the workspace continuously, alerts when junk creeps back,
and catches the binges (a memory-loss receipt = the trainer catching the AI
snacking at full price).
- Measured basis [ANCHOR]: the watcher already exists (runs every 60 seconds,
  local-only, zero network calls); the recurring auto-diet is on the roadmap.
- The shareable artifacts: before/after weigh-in card ("22 MB gone in one
  command"), binge receipt ("your AI binged 207k tokens last night").

CONTRAST LINE: "Caveman makes your AI talk less. Jev makes your AI think
faster. Sweap makes your AI weigh less."

CONSTRAINTS (fixed): no ads, no cold outreach, product-led only; every public
number measured or stamped hypothetical; local-only privacy is a quiet README
section, not a slogan; complementary to caveman/jev, never attacks them.

    Gut-check done. This one has real bones because the weigh-in is a

    G1 - THE BUS TEST: It composes into ONE story, but only if you never see
    Layer 2's numerology. The throughline is literally a sentence: "Your AI
    is fat. It costs you to carry that. Weigh it now, keep it off." That's
    one feeling, and it's the right one (health metaphor, trainer everywhere).
    
    The sentence where attention dies, named: "one observed day had 207,566
    tokens billed at full price from 3 memory-loss events; cache hit on the
    good lane is 96.3%". That is where a non-technical person checks out.
    "Tokens" is not money to them, 207,566 is not magnitude to them, and the
    96.3% hedge tells them "ok this is for engineers." You've told a health
    story and then dropped them into an engineering footnote. The sting
    should be in dollars or in plain "re-reads all the junk," never in raw
    token counts.
    
    The other subtle seam: Layers 1 and 2 mix metaphors (weight + money). It
    survives because "carrying charge" is a weight-flavored finance word and
    the trainer knits them back together, but only if Layer 2 stays a cost
    story and doesn't become a statistics lecture. Keep the health metaphor
    in charge of the writing, let money be the number underneath.
    
    G2 - WEAKEST LAYER: The sting, and not for the reason you think. It's not
