The program

Three drills.
Run them for life.

Not a cleaner you run once: a trainer that stays. Everything runs on your machine, reads your AI's own memory, and reverses with proof.

Drill 01

The weigh-in

One command reads your AI's memory and prints the slip: dead weight, duplicate thinking, resting tool exhaust. --dry-run measures and touches nothing. Thirty seconds, your numbers.

3 sets · read-only · daily
Drill 02

The cut

sweap.it finds and cleans the dead weight, reversibly. Every row is backed up and hash-verified before anything moves, and one command puts every byte back. A rehearsal on a copy runs first. Nothing deletes on faith.

gated · reversible · monthly
Drill 03

Keep it off

The trainer watches the scale daily. Quiet days say nothing. When junk creeps back it flags you: "Junk is creeping back, up 8.3 MB since Monday. One command: sweap diet." It finds and cleans. It does not pretend to prevent.

automated · flags, never nags
The price board

What the extra weight costs

After every memory lapse, your AI re-reads the whole pile. Re-read tokens bill at the cache-miss rate instead of the cache-hit rate. That gap is the carrying charge, and it is arithmetic on list prices, not a prediction.

Gym rules

Posted by the door. They are properties of the code, not promises.
1No network, provably. Zero imports that can open a socket. Unplug the cable; sweap.it works identically. Verified under a hard firewall.
2No dependencies. Nothing to install, nothing to poison. The whole toolkit is Python's standard library.
3Reversible with proof. Every cut is quarantined with full-column hashes first; restore is byte-verified, drilled against schema changes.
4Never first-run. Destructive steps need a staged manifest and the same id typed twice. A crash rolls back, loudly.
5Honest receipts. Wire savings are stamped $0.00 where they are $0.00. Hypothetical figures are labeled, never blended with measured facts.
6Security-audited. 18-point baseline audit passed; findings fixed same-day. The scan commands ship in the repo: re-run them yourself.
