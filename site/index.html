THE PROGRAM

A trainer, not a cleaner you run once.

Three moves, all from your own machine, all reversible. The default command touches nothing.

01 / WEIGH

The weigh-in

One command reads your AI's memory and prints the receipt: dead weight, duplicate thinking, resting tool exhaust. sweap diet --dry-run measures and modifies nothing. Your numbers, your machine, thirty seconds.

02 / DIET

The diet

sweap finds and cleans the dead weight, reversibly. Every row is backed up and hash-verified before anything is touched, and one command puts every byte back. A live rehearsal on a copy runs first. Nothing deletes on faith.

03 / KEEP IT OFF

The trainer

A daily scale check watches the weight. Quiet days say nothing. When junk creeps back it flags you: "Junk is creeping back, up 8.3 MB since Monday. One command: sweap diet." It finds and cleans; it does not pretend to prevent.

THE LAPSE PRICE

Your junk has a price. It's charged every time your AI forgets.

After a memory lapse, everything gets re-read. Re-read tokens bill at the cache-miss rate instead of the cache-hit rate. That gap is the carrying charge, and it is arithmetic on list prices, not a prediction.

The surcharge on GLM-4.6 at z.ai list prices (Oct 2026):
$0.49 per 1M junk tokens, per lapse
re-read at $0.60/M instead of $0.11/M · a 5.45× surcharge
On our own machine, that mechanism was real: 49% of all paid uncached tokens were break re-reads (measured, one host, frozen blind audit).

The local-only warranty, as a property of the code

✓No network, provably. Zero imports that can open a socket. Unplug the cable; sweap works identically. Verified under a hard firewall: every tool passes.
✓No dependencies. Nothing to install, nothing to poison. The whole toolkit is Python's standard library.
✓Reversible with proof. Every diet quarantines full-column hashed copies first; restore is byte-verified, drilled against schema changes.
✓Never first-run. Destructive steps need a staged manifest and the same id typed twice. A crash rolls back, loudly.
✓Honest receipts. Wire savings are stamped $0.00 where they are $0.00. Hypothetical figures are labeled, never blended with measured facts.
✓Security-audited. 18-point baseline audit passed; findings fixed same-day. The scan commands ship in the repo so you can re-run them.
