## Why the Caveman story worked (deconstruction, 7 mechanics)

1. **The joke was the demo.** "Make agents talk like cavemen" is absurd, so people
   tried it in 30 seconds; the savings made them stay. One command, instant proof.
2. **Name = thesis.** Caveman: primitive speech, few tokens. The name performs the
   promise. Tagline continues it ("Why many token when few do trick").
3. **Form = function.** Every heading on the site is in-voice; reading the site IS
   experiencing compression. The medium demonstrates the message.
4. **Community became the marketing.** Users started replying in caveman; the brand
   was fun to quote, so it recruited its own evangelists (HN thread, 904 pts).
5. **Rigor underneath the joke.** Paired benchmarks, JetBrains study, Adobe citation,
   CaveBench, and the explicit disclaimer "Community reactions are not benchmarks."
   The joke earned trust by backing itself with receipts.
6. **The arc: joke → OSS → stars → press → Cloud ($300/mo) → enterprise/VPC.**
   Classic open-core: the free joke is distribution, the cloud is the business.
   Press hook: "companies are making AI talk like cavemen" writes its own headlines
   (Fortune tokenmaxxing piece, 404 Media, Hackaday, Decrypt, BNR founder story).
7. **Escalation path for skeptics:** stars and memes get attention; benchmarks and
   "no detectable quality difference" close it. Story first, proof second, both real.
